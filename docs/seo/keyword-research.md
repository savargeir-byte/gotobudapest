# GoToBudapest: keyword research and content strategy

Prepared 2026-11-17. Evidence-only research. No Search Console, no keyword tool, no volumes, no difficulty scores.

## 0. Method, limits and how to read this file

**Evidence labels used throughout**
- **OBSERVED** = seen in a search-result list returned by the search tool, in a fetched page, or in the live marketplace API (URL given).
- **INFERENCE** = my reasoning from observed facts. Not proven.

**What I could and could not see (read this first)**
- The search tool returns, per query, a list of result titles/URLs (roughly the top 15-30) plus a short machine summary. It does **not** show Google autosuggest, "People also ask" boxes, featured snippets, map packs, video carousels, shopping units or ads. I never observed any of those. Wherever this file says "suggestion phrasing", it means phrasing taken from result titles and page headings that the search returned, **not** real autocomplete.
- The tool is described as US-only, so results are US-localised. UK/EU/ISK-market SERPs may differ. INFERENCE.
- Result order from this tool is not guaranteed to equal Google's order. I treat it as "who appears for this query", not as a ranking position.
- I could not check whether any gotobuda.com page is indexed or ranks for anything (the `site:` operator returned only unrelated sites: OBSERVED, search "site:gotobuda.com Budapest"). Current rankings are unknown.
- Demand is expressed only qualitatively. "Many pages target this" is not the same as "many people search it". I did not measure demand.
- Competitor pages were mostly judged from titles/snippets. I fetched in full only: budapest-trip.com Christmas and Budapest Card guides, and bathsbudapest.com prices.
- Prices quoted from third parties conflict heavily between sources. None are safe to publish without checking the official source on the day. Examples are flagged below.

**Site facts used (all OBSERVED, live fetch of https://gotobudapest.vercel.app on 2026-11-17)**
- Sitemap = 303 URLs under https://www.gotobuda.com: 220 attraction pages, 35 neighbourhoods, 8 guides (+ index), 4 experiences, 1 /tours, 1 /visitor-guides index, 7 transportation, 5 gastronomy, 6 culture, 9 /visit pages. No Vienna, Christmas, itinerary-1/2-day, Hospital-in-the-Rock-guide or rainy-day page exists as a standalone URL.
- **/tours is the only commercial URL in the sitemap.** `/tours?category=baths` returns `noindex, follow` with canonical to /tours. Product pages (`/tours/viator/35015P1`) are `noindex, follow`. So every commercial query currently has exactly one indexable landing page, /tours (about 200-250 words of editorial text, no FAQ).
- Only guides and a few neighbourhood/experience pages carry FAQPage schema (guides/danube-cruises, budapest-thermal-baths, budapest-in-3-days, budapest-bars-nightlife, neighbourhoods/jewish-quarter, experiences/danube). The attraction pages I checked (szechenyi, hungarian-parliament, hospital-in-the-rock, house-of-terror-museum, st-stephens-basilica, dohany-street-synagogue, hungarian-state-opera) and visit/budapest-card-guide and visit/seasonal-budapest have **no** FAQPage schema (budapest-card-guide displays two FAQ questions but has no FAQPage markup).
- Every page I checked links to the generic /tours only (a few link to `/tours?category=...`, which is noindex). No attraction page links to or embeds a specific product.
- The site deliberately shows no prices on attraction pages ("does not show ticket prices").

**Inventory warnings (OBSERVED)**
1. `categories` tags are loose: the "Unlimited Prosecco, Beer, Aperol & Limoncello Spritz Cruise" is tagged cruises/baths/walking/food/tickets/sightseeing, so it appears in every category. Category filters should not be used to build a curated module. Curate product IDs instead.
2. `q=` is a fuzzy search. Several queries return unrelated results. A hand-picked product list per page is safer than a live `q=` call.
3. The Tiqets "Budapest Card" listing is a reseller product. I did not verify it is the official BKK card. INFERENCE: check before recommending it on the Budapest Card page.
